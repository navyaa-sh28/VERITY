## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an evidence-first brand governance environment engineered for enterprise compliance, truth-verification, and meticulous regulatory oversight. The visual posture reflects institutional gravitas, calm authority, and judicial clarity.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Rigorous & Verifiable:** Every assertion is anchored to auditable proof; UI primitives favor systematic substantiation over decoration.
- **Calm Authority:** High-density governance without visual fatigue. Quiet, muted tones and generous spatial allocation reduce cognitive burden during high-stakes audits.
- **Institutional Trust:** Evoking the permanence and precision of legal registries, institutional vaults, and technical ledgers.

### Design Movement
The interface employs a **Corporate / Modern Editorial** framework grounded in subtle structural framing, low-contrast ghost borders, and tactile verification states. Depth is expressed through structural layers and distinct status semantics rather than theatrical drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep blues against clinical teals, warm parchment neutrals, and functional verification markers.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#152A35`):** Serves as the primary institutional anchor—used for primary actions, structural navigation, key metrics, and dominant headings.
- **Secondary Teal (`#218C84`):** Represents dynamic governance, active verification paths, and secondary navigational accents.
- **Accent Gold (`#D4A85A`):** Used sparingly for critical attention triggers, outdated status flags, and compliance review warnings.
- **Background Off-white (`#F7F7F5`):** The foundational substrate across all viewports, preventing harsh contrast glare while retaining sterile neutrality.
- **Surface Mint/Tint (`#EAF3F1`):** Applied to secondary containers, verified evidence wells, callouts, and nested calculation panels.
- **Pure Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority operational planes, actionable cards, modals, and verified claim containers.

### Evidence & Verification Status Tokens
- **Supported (`#3F8F6B`):** Conveys fully verified claims, authenticated citations, and compliant status.
- **Contradicted (`#B85C55`):** Flags conflicting claims, invalid regulatory claims, and immediate non-compliance.
- **Outdated (`#D4A85A`):** Marks expired attestations, stale documentation, and impending renewal dates.
- **Unsupported (`#218C84`):** Denotes assertions lacking source material or orphaned data points awaiting attachment.
- **Human Review (`#152A35`):** Signals an ambiguous determination escalated to manual compliance officer review.

## Typography

The typography architecture balances structural presence with clinical legibility.

- **Headlines (Montserrat):** Set in clean geometric cuts with tight tracking at larger sizes. Used for enterprise dashboard titles, audit section markers, and high-level verification indicators.
- **Body & Metadata (Open Sans):** Unassuming, open counters with high horizontal clarity. Provides sustained readability across dense multi-page policy diffs, legal citations, and audit trail tables.
- **Code & Fingerprints (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced tokening reserved for cryptographic hashes, timestamp manifests, revision IDs, and machine-parsed compliance clauses.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for dense audit surfaces, document comparison panels, and telemetry splits.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `margin-lg` (3rem), `gutter-lg` (2rem). Supports multi-pane interfaces such as side-by-side claim adjudication.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Comparison layouts stack or switch to tabbed views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Workspaces stack into single-column verification feeds.

### Spatial Cadence
- Content cards, verification logs, and audit panels use `space-lg` to `space-xl` internally to preserve an unhurried, calm presentation despite complex data schemas.
- Micro-elements (such as status badges, verification chips, and inline citation pills) rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compact cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately minimizes intrusive drop shadows, leaning instead on low-contrast structural outlines, tonal layering, and surface stacking to communicate depth.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Canvas Base:** `#F7F7F5` sits at base level zero.
- **Elevated Surfaces:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) represents interactive, actionable objects—cards, modals, drawer sheets, and data tables.
- **Subordinate Wells & Panels:** `#EAF3F1` denotes read-only evidence pools, contextual quotes, and verification rationale wells recessed inside white cards.

### Outlines & Borders
- Cards and structural sections utilize a subtle outline of `1px solid rgba(21, 42, 53, 0.08)` (a muted slate-gray derived from the primary navy).
- Hover states and active selections reinforce focus by transitioning borders to `rgba(33, 140, 132, 0.4)` without adjusting spatial boundaries.

### Ambient Shadows
- Modals, context menus, and detached verification overlays use an ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(21, 42, 53, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(21, 42, 53, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft-cornered geometry that tempers institutional strictness with contemporary enterprise ergonomics.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Standardized on `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) to `rounded-lg` (1rem) for soft, structured surfaces that enclose compliance records without sharp tension.
- **Internal Wells & Media Containers:** Fixed at `rounded-md` (0.5rem) to preserve clean internal nesting inside outer cards.
- **Badges, Chips, & Status Indicators:** Use fully circular or pill contours (`rounded-full`) to immediately distinguish analytical tokens and metadata flags from rectangular data containers.
- **Form Fields & Buttons:** Standardized at `rounded-md` (0.5rem) to ensure clear interactive affordance and structural stability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#152A35` background with white text, 0.5rem roundedness, uppercase or semi-bold Open Sans label (`label-md`). Hover transitions to `#1E3A49`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with a `1px solid #152A35` border and `#152A35` label. Hover transitions to `#EAF3F1` fill.
- **Verification CTA:** Secondary Teal fill (`#218C84`) with pure white text, reserved for confirming compliance or attesting evidence.
- **Ghost:** Transparent fill with slate text; relies strictly on hover background fill `rgba(21, 42, 53, 0.04)`.

### Evidence Badges & Status Indicator Chips
- **Geometry:** Height 24px, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `2px 10px`, typography set to `label-sm`.
- **Tone Variations:** Light-tinted background (12% opacity of token color) paired with a solid status-colored label and an optional leading 6px circular dot indicator:
  - *Supported:* Background `#E8F4EE`, text `#3F8F6B`, dot `#3F8F6B`.
  - *Contradicted:* Background `#F7EBEA`, text `#B85C55`, dot `#B85C55`.
  - *Outdated:* Background `#FAF4E8`, text `#967129`, dot `#D4A85A`.
  - *Unsupported:* Background `#E4F1F0`, text `#1B6E67`, dot `#218C84`.
  - *Human Review:* Background `#E3E8EB`, text `#152A35`, dot `#152A35`.

### Audit Trail Lists
- **Structure:** Vertical timelines featuring a continuous `1px` subtle rail (`rgba(21, 42, 53, 0.1)`).
- **Log Item:** Minimal padding (`space-md`), containing user avatar or service icon, action verb in `body-sm`, highlighted timestamp in `label-sm` muted slate, and linked record hash in `code-sm`.
- **Interactivity:** Expandable rows showing granular cryptographic metadata, delta changes, and verified source documents.

### Side-by-Side Comparison Cards
- **Construction:** Dual white containers (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border (`rgba(21, 42, 53, 0.08)`) and `rounded-xl` corners.
- **Original vs. Claim Layout:** Left panel displays raw ingest copy; right panel highlights parsed assertions, inline compliance tags, and citation linkage bars.
- **Diff Highlighting:** Removed or contradicting phrases highlighted in soft red tint (`#F7EBEA`), verified assertions outlined in soft mint tint (`#EAF3F1`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height 40px, pure white surface, border `1px solid rgba(21, 42, 53, 0.16)`, text `#152A35`. Focus ring transitions to `#218C84` with `0 0 0 2px rgba(33, 140, 132, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Controlled inputs styled with `#152A35` active fill, white check/dot glyphs, and smooth border transitions.
